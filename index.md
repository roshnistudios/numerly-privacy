# Privacy Policy — Numerly

**Developer:** Roshni Studios  
**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

Numerly ("the game") is a number-connecting puzzle game made by Roshni Studios. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

## What stays on your device
Your progress — levels played, stars, coins, gems, skins, and settings such as sound — is saved on your device. The game has no accounts of its own, and we (Roshni Studios) do not run a server and do not receive or store your game data. We collect no personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

**Optional cloud save:** if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, your progress is also saved to your Google Play Games account (Saved Games), so you can restore it on a new phone. That copy is stored and handled by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Who the game shares data with
Only the services built into the game, and only as needed for what they do: Google AdMob (ads), Google Play (purchases) and, if you use it, Google Play Games (cloud save). We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone else.

## How data is kept safe
Because we hold no personal data ourselves, there is nothing on our side to leak. The services above send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Your progress stays in the game's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

## How long data is kept, and how to delete it
Game data on your phone is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data (Android: Settings → Apps → Numerly → Storage → Clear data); that deletes it. Cloud save is kept in your Google Play Games account until you delete it there (Google Play Games app → Settings → Delete Play Games account and data). Ad and purchase data kept by Google can be managed or deleted from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com. We have no account system, so there is no other account to delete. For any request, write to us at official.roshni.studios@gmail.com.

## Ads
The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technologies. That information is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not receive this information and we do not sell it. Rewarded ads only play when you tap the button for them.

## Purchases
Optional in-app purchases (coins, gems, theme sets, Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only learn that a purchase was completed so we can give you what you bought.

## Your choices
Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent to personalized ads through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from **Privacy & About → Ad privacy settings**. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on our store page, with a new "last updated" date.

## Contact

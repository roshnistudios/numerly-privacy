# Privacy Policy — Numerly

**Developer:** Roshni Studios  
**Last updated:** October 25, 2026

Numerly ("the game") is a number-connecting puzzle game made by Roshni Studios. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

## What stays on your device
Your progress — unlocked levels, stars, coins, hints, purchases made with in-game coins, and settings such as sound — is saved only on your device. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we do not run a server that receives your game data. Uninstalling the game removes it.

## Ads
The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and use information from your device such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location, and may use cookies or similar technologies. That information is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not receive this information and we do not sell it. Rewarded ads only play when you tap the button for them.

## Purchases
Optional in-app purchases (coins, Remove Ads) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only learn that a purchase was completed so we can give you what you bought.

## Your choices
Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent to personalized ads through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from **Privacy & About → Ad privacy settings**. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app and on our store page, with a new "last updated" date.
